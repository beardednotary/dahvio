Frequently Asked Questions

Is this app official?

No. BriefBuilder is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the U.S. Air Force, the Department of Defense, or myEval. Always check your EPB against current Air Force guidance and your rater's direction.

How do I get a statement from a log entry?

Open the entry and tap Write. The Statement Writer generates two options with character counts. Tap the one you want and it is added to the EPB block for that entry's performance area. You can regenerate if neither option fits.

The statement has a detail wrong. What do I do?

The writer works only from what you typed, and it can still get things wrong or word them more strongly than the facts support. Read every statement before you use it and correct anything inaccurate. You are responsible for what goes on your EPB. Entries with specific numbers and a clear result produce the most accurate statements.

It says the block is full. Why?

Each of the four performance area blocks holds up to 350 characters, matching the EPB. If adding a statement would go over, the app stops you. Open the Builder tab, remove a statement from that block, and add the new one.

How do I get my statements into myEval?

In the Builder tab, copy a block to your clipboard, then paste it into the matching section in myEval or on AF Form 716. BriefBuilder does not connect to myEval or any Air Force system.

How many free AI writes do I get?

Logging is free and unlimited. The AI writer includes five free uses in total. Each time you generate or regenerate statements counts as one. Your Profile tab shows how many you have used. An upgrade unlocks unlimited writing.

I upgraded but the app still shows the free limit.

Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple or Google account you used to buy. If it still doesn't unlock, email the address below with your purchase receipt.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by DahVio Studios. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions.

The writer shows an error or says to try again.

The AI writer needs an internet connection. Check your connection and try again. If you see a message about too many requests, wait a minute. Failed attempts do not count against your free writes.

What should I never put in an entry?

Never enter classified information, controlled unclassified information (CUI), non-releasable operational details, or personal information about other people. When you use the AI writer, the entry's text is sent off your device for processing. See the privacy policy for exactly what is sent and where.

Where is my data, and is it backed up?

Your log, statements, and EPB blocks are stored only on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync, so deleting the app or switching phones removes them. Copy your blocks somewhere safe before you change devices.

How do I change my rank, AFSC, or erase everything?

Open the Profile tab and use Reset App Data. It erases your profile, log, statements, and blocks from the device and returns you to setup, where you can enter a new rank and AFSC. This cannot be undone, so copy your blocks first.

Does it support the Space Force or officers?

No. BriefBuilder is built for the Air Force enlisted evaluation system, AB through CMSgt, in the Regular Air Force, Air Force Reserve, and Air National Guard.
