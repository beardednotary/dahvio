2026 Standards

Waist-to-height standards by branch

DoWI 1308.03 (effective 31 August 2026) set one ceiling for every Service: a ratio under 0.55. A Service may be stricter, and each one decides how to round and what happens next.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the military waist-to-height ratio standard for 2026?

DoWI 1308.03, effective 31 August 2026, caps every Service at a waist-to-height ratio under 0.55, and lets a Service set a stricter one. The Marine Corps standard is 0.52 or less. The Army standard is under 0.55. The Navy's first step passes 0.5499 or lower. The Air Force scores the ratio for up to 20 PFA points: 0.49 or lower earns all 20 and 0.60 or higher earns none.

How do you calculate waist-to-height ratio?

Divide your waist circumference by your height, both in inches. A 35-inch waist at 70 inches tall is a ratio of 0.50.

Where do you measure your waist for the military waist-to-height ratio?

DoWI 1308.03 sets the DoD method: midway between the lowest rib and the top of the hip bone, usually at or just above the belly button, standing and breathing normally, with a non-stretch tape parallel to the floor. The Marine Corps measures at the navel. Your command's measurement is the record.

What is the maximum waist for my height?

At 70 inches tall the largest waist that meets the standard is 36.0 inches for the Marine Corps, 38.0 inches for the Navy's first step, and 38.4 inches for the Army. For all 20 Air Force PFA points it is 34.6 inches. The chart on this page lists every height from 58 to 80 inches.

What happens if you are over the waist-to-height standard?

In the Marine Corps and the Navy, a Marine or Sailor over the standard gets a body fat evaluation, and only fails body composition if they are also over the body fat limit. In the Army, under AD 2026-13, the waist-to-height ratio is the standard itself and no tape test can be used to challenge it. In the Air Force, a higher ratio costs PFA points, and 0.60 or higher scores zero on the component, which makes the PFA Unsatisfactory.

Did the Army get rid of the tape test?

Yes. Army Directive 2026-13 makes a waist-to-height ratio under 0.55 the Army body composition standard for every Soldier, and says no tape test or other body fat method can be used to challenge a WHtR result.

Does rounding change my waist-to-height result?

It can at the limit. The Marine Corps rounds height and waist down to the nearest half inch. The Navy rounds height up and waist down to the nearest half inch. The Air Force rounds the ratio to two decimals. This calculator applies each branch's rule and shows what was recorded.

Is this an official military body composition calculator?

No. It is an independent tool that applies each branch's published rule. Your command's measurement and assessment are the record.
