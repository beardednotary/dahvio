FAQ

A few quick answers.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, and your record stays on your device. Vessels, if you join one, uses an anonymous sign-in: no name, no email.

Why does it want Health access?

Only if you turn on Health sync. It writes your logged water to Apple Health and does not read your existing Health data.

What does Preserved unlock?

The Ledger, The Vessel and The Record widgets, Apple Watch complications, Vessels, custom quick-log amounts, full history and weekly insights, the full notice archive, and alternate app icons, through one lifetime purchase.

Is this medical advice?

No. The skull is not a physician. If a condition affects how much you should drink, ask one.
