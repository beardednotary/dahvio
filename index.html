Tools for people whose jobs have standards.

DahVio Studios is a one-person studio in Long Beach, California. Most of what I build is scoring and reference work — a service member's PT test, a hazmat placard, a firefighter's CPAT, a promotion board. Where there's a published standard, the standard is the product: if the number is wrong, the app is worthless, so the numbers come straight from the regulation.

The rest is what a working studio picks up along the way — browser extensions, earlier apps, a few web properties. All of it shipped.

About

I'm Ray, founder of DahVio Studios — named after my daughters, Dahlia and Violet. Marine Corps veteran, single dad, solo developer.

I started with a PT calculator because the ones I could find were wrong, or buried in ads, or wanted a subscription to tell me a number the instruction already publishes for free. Real users sent feedback, I iterated, and the rest followed from the same instinct: promotion boards and fitness standards are high-stakes moments, and the tools weren't built by someone who'd been through them.

Everything on this page is shipped and in someone's hands.
