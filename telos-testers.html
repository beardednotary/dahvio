Telos · Tester brief

Telos

Telos is a focus timer that only pays off when you put your phone down. I want to find out if that idea holds up for someone who isn't me, and mostly whether you're still opening it on day three.

iPhone iOS 15 or newer One week No account
01

What it is

You pick a block of time. A small guild of adventurers heads out for exactly that long, and you get on with your actual work. When you're back, you find out what they brought home.

The rule the whole thing is built on: opening the app doesn't move anything forward. Putting it down does.

Nothing piles up while you watch the timer. There's nothing to tap and nothing to collect. That's backwards from most games on your phone, and it's the thing I'm testing.

One more thing to know up front. Every run starts at 100% integrity. Staring at the timer costs a bit. Opening the app mid-run costs more. Locking your phone and walking away costs nothing. Integrity barely touches the resources you earn, but it matters a lot for rare finds.

02

Installing it

Send me the email on your Apple ID and I'll add you. You'll get a TestFlight invite.

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store if you don't have it.
  2. Open the invite email on your phone and tap the link.
  3. Install Telos from inside TestFlight.

You need iOS 15 or newer. The widget and Siri need iOS 16. New builds come through TestFlight, so you'll get a notification if I push a fix.

Allow notifications when it asks. There's only one: it tells you when a run is done.

03

Starting a session fast

The dispatch screen is there when you want to pick things. Most of the time you'll just want the same as last time, so there are four faster ways in. I'd like to know which ones you actually use.

  • SEND AGAIN on the home screen shows your last three dispatches. One tap each.
  • The app icon. Press and hold it for the same three.
  • The widget. Add Telos to your home screen or lock screen. The medium size shows all three.
  • Siri and Shortcuts. Say “Dispatch in Telos.” Or set up an automation in the Shortcuts app that runs Telos's Dispatch action when your work Focus turns on. Then turning on Focus sends the squad out.

All of these start the run right away. If you hit one by accident, RECALL SQUAD brings them back.

While the squad's out, the countdown shows on your lock screen. Looking at it is free. Only opening Telos counts.

04

Your week

Use it for work you were going to do anyway. Please don't make up sessions for my sake. That won't tell me whether it holds up in a normal week.

Over seven days, if you can:

  • Four or five real sessions, whatever length fits the work.
  • At least one short one (15 or 25 minutes) and at least one over an hour. The game treats those really differently.
  • One where you pick up your phone partway through on purpose, so you can see what integrity does.

That's all. If you stop after two days, tell me. That's useful to know too.

05

Things I already know

So you don't spend time reporting stuff that's missing on purpose.

Missing Why
App blockingTelos can't see what else you open, and it doesn't pretend to.
Accounts or syncIt makes no network calls at all. That's on purpose.
Anything to buyThere are no purchases yet.
New content after the SpireThe last area opens after 5 to 10 weeks of daily long sessions. It has an ending, and after that your hours just keep building the Spire. That's on purpose too.
What I don't know

Whether the pacing is right. The prices were checked against the game's own math, never against an actual person. If progress feels way too slow or suspiciously fast, that's the most useful thing you can tell me.

06

What I need back

Six questions at the end of the week. Short answers are fine, and blunt ones help me more than nice ones. Send them wherever we've been talking.

1. Did you get the point in the first minute?

If not, where did it lose you?

2. Were you still opening it on day three?

If you stopped, what was going on that day?

3. On a day you meant to work, what stopped you starting a session?

Even if it's “nothing, I just didn't.”

4. Did it change how you actually worked?

Or was it decoration on sessions you'd have done anyway?

5. Did anything feel like a chore?

Especially anything where the app seemed to want something from you.

6. If it disappeared tomorrow, what would you miss?

“Nothing” is a real answer.

If something breaks

Send me the build number. It's at the very bottom of FACILITIES.

07

Sending your save

At the end of the week, tap FACILITIES at the bottom of the home screen, then SAVE DATA, then EXPORT A BACKUP. Send me the file however's easiest.

It's a plain JSON file with your guild, every session you ran, and the note you typed before each one. Open it first if you want to see exactly what's in it. If any of those notes are about work you'd rather I didn't see, don't send it. Your answers to the six questions matter more than the file.

What the app collects

Nothing leaves your phone. No analytics, no account, no server, and Telos makes no network calls. It counts how many times it was opened and on which days, just so that exported file can show whether you came back. It all stays on your device until you send it, and deleting the app deletes all of it.
