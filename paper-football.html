How to play
  • Swipe to flick. Tap just behind it to nudge.
  • Stop it hanging off the far edge: touchdown, 6.
  • Slide it off the table: turnover.
  • Four flicks per drive.
  • After a touchdown, flick it through the finger posts for 1.
  • Fan 10: ten kicks, the fan gets stronger every kick.
  • Streak: every make moves the kick back and turns the fan up. One miss ends it.
  • Long Bomb: no fan, every make moves you back 3 inches. Three misses and you're out.
  • After a kicking run, Challenge a friend sends a link: they get the same fan, one shot to beat you.
  • Daily Kick: five kicks, the same for everyone today. One shot, then share your score. Play every day to build a streak.
