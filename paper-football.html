How to play
  • Swipe to flick. Tap just behind it to nudge.
  • Stop it hanging off the far edge: touchdown, 6.
  • Slide it off the table: turnover.
  • Four flicks per drive.
  • After a touchdown, flick it through the finger posts for 1.
