Privacy Policy

Paper Football is a free game you play in your web browser at dahvio.com. This policy explains what the game saves on your device, what the site measures, and what happens when you send feedback.

Effective: October 16, 2026

The short version: there's no account and no sign-up. Your settings and best score stay in your browser. The site uses Google Analytics to count visits, and anything you write in the feedback form is only sent if you tap Send.

1. Who We Are

Paper Football is made by DahVio Studios, a solo developer studio based in Long Beach, California, operated by Ray (ray@dahvio.com).

2. What Stays on Your Device

The game saves a few things in your browser's local storage so they're still there next time you play:

  • Your Tune settings (table grip, flick power, kick power)
  • Your computer difficulty, desk fan setting, and sound setting
  • Your best Fan Challenge score

Games in progress and scores aren't saved anywhere. You can erase the saved settings at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

None of this is sent to DahVio Studios or anyone else, unless you send feedback (see section 4), which includes your current settings so Ray can match feedback to how you played.

3. Analytics and Cookies

The game page uses Google Analytics, the same as the rest of dahvio.com, to understand how many people visit and play. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and collects information such as:

  • Pages you view and how long you stay
  • Your device type, browser, and screen size
  • Your approximate location (country or city level), based on your IP address
  • The site or link that brought you here

DahVio Studios sees this as aggregated reports, not as information about you personally. Google processes this data under Google's Privacy Policy. You can block it with your browser's cookie or tracker settings, or with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The game works the same either way.

4. Feedback You Send

The game menu has a Send feedback form. Nothing in it leaves your device unless you tap Send. When you do, it sends:

  • The answers you picked and any note you wrote
  • Your name, only if you choose to enter one
  • Your computer difficulty, Tune settings, current or last score, and best Fan Challenge score
  • Your screen size and browser's device description (for example, the iPhone and browser version)

Feedback is delivered through Formspree, a form service that stores submissions and emails them to Ray. It's used only to improve the game. Please don't include anything sensitive in your note. To have your feedback deleted, email ray@dahvio.com.

5. Fonts and Hosting

The game's fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser information when it delivers them. The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard request logs, such as IP address and browser, to run and secure the service.

6. No Ads, No Selling

Paper Football shows no ads. DahVio Studios doesn't sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

7. Do Not Track

The site doesn't change its behavior in response to browser Do Not Track signals. To limit analytics, use the options in section 3.

8. Children's Privacy

Paper Football is a general-audience game and isn't directed at children under 13. The game doesn't need any personal information to play. If you believe a child has sent us their name through the feedback form, email us and we'll delete it.

9. Your Choices

You can ask what feedback we have from you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing ray@dahvio.com. Settings saved on your device are yours to clear at any time.

10. Changes to This Policy

If the game's data practices change, for example if leaderboards are added, this policy will be updated with a new effective date.
