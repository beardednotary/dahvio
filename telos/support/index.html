Telos support

Telos is a focus timer for iPhone. You pick a block of time, a small guild heads out for exactly that long, and you get on with your work. If something isn't working, or doesn't make sense, this page should help, and if it doesn't, email me.

Get in touch

Email telos@dahvio.com with bugs, questions or ideas. I make Telos myself and read every message.

The quickest way is from inside the app: open Settings & backup with the gear at camp, then Help & about, then Send feedback. That starts an email with your build and iOS version already filled in.

Reporting a bug

Say what you did, what happened and what you expected. Include the build number, which is at the very bottom of Settings & backup.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Telos has no accounts, and it makes no network connections of its own. Everything stays on your phone.

I didn't get a notification when my squad came back.

Telos asks for permission the first time you send a squad out. If you said no, turn it back on in the iPhone Settings app under Notifications › Telos. The run finishes on time either way, so open Telos to collect the report. The only notification is the one that tells you a run is over, plus caravan notices if you schedule them yourself.

What lowers Integrity?

Every run starts at 100%. Watching the countdown costs a little, and opening Telos mid-run costs more. Locking your phone and walking away costs nothing, and glancing at the Lock Screen countdown is free. Integrity barely changes the resources you earn, but it matters a lot for rare finds.

My squad came back with almost nothing.

Each destination has a minimum time. Come back before it and the squad brings only scraps. Pick a shorter destination for short sessions, and save the far ones for long blocks.

I started a run by accident.

Tap Recall squad on the session screen to bring them back.

The widget or Siri isn't showing up.

Telos runs on iOS 15, but Siri needs iOS 16, and the widget and the Lock Screen countdown need iOS 16.2 or newer.

Can Telos block other apps?

No. Telos can't see what else you open, and it doesn't pretend to.

How do I move my guild to a new phone?

If you restore the new phone from an iCloud or computer backup, your guild comes with it. Otherwise, on the old phone go to Settings & backup › Save & restore › Export a backup and send the file to yourself. On the new phone, use Choose a backup file in the same place. Telos shows both guilds side by side before it replaces anything.

Telos says my guild could not be opened.

Telos never deletes a save it can't read. It keeps the old one on your phone and lets you save a copy before carrying on with a new guild. Email that copy to telos@dahvio.com and I'll try to repair it. If you exported a backup earlier, you can also restore that.

How do I delete my data?

Delete Telos from your phone. Backups you exported, and your phone's own backups, stay until you delete them. The privacy policy has the details.
