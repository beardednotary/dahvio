More about it

Where they go depends on how long you've got

Every place has a minimum. Come back before it and the squad brings home scraps. So one 90 minute block really is different from three 30s, but nothing in the game tells you which one you should be working. If you only ever do 25s, that's fine.

  1. Mosswood Verge

    10 min

    Overgrown perimeter. Low risk, quick returns.

  2. Blackstone Hollow

    25 min

    Collapsed extraction works. Alloy-rich, slow going.

  3. The Cinder Archive

    40 min

    A burned library that never finished burning.

  4. Riftline Descent

    60 min

    Past the last marked map. Only a long run gets anywhere.

There's one more past those. You'll have to get there yourself.

Who goes depends on how you work

Nobody's strictly better. Each class suits a different length of session, so the way you already work picks your squad.

Recon

Fast, light, in and out. Built for short bursts of work.

Excels on runs of 10-30 min

Vanguard

Heavy, patient, hard to stop. Built for long hauls.

Excels on runs of 60 min and up

Archivist

Reads the ruins instead of looting them. Produces intel.

Excels on runs of 45 min and up

Artificer

Strips salvage for parts. Finds gear others walk past.

Excels on runs of 25-75 min

Quartermaster

No specialism, no bad days. Steady on any length of run.

Steady on any run length

Opening the app doesn't help them

Nothing piles up while you watch the timer. There's nothing to tap and nothing to collect. Every run starts at 100% Integrity. Staring at the timer costs a bit, opening the app mid-run costs more, and locking your phone costs nothing. Integrity barely touches the resources you earn, but it matters a lot for rare finds.

You get one notification, when the run's done. Then you read the report and decide what to spend it on: gear, recruits, the forge, your camp.

What it won't do

  • No account, no ads, no analytics. Telos makes no network connections of its own, and it works offline.
  • What you write stays on your phone unless you choose to send it somewhere. Share cards never include your task or reflection notes.
  • No streaks and no reminders you didn't set up yourself. Take a month off and nothing's lost.
  • You'll never be able to buy progress. No boosts, no skipping a session, no energy. Focus is the only thing that moves the game.
  • You can export your whole save to a file any time. Deleting the app deletes everything it keeps on your phone.
