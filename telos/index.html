Telos

The focus timer that rewards you for leaving it alone.

Pick a block of time. A small guild heads out for exactly that long, and you get on with your actual work. When you're back, you find out what they brought home.

More about it

One run, start to finish

Telos is a focus timer first. Ignore the game completely and you still have a quiet timer and a record of what you worked on. Your first run comes already filled in, so it's one tap.

  1. Plan it

    Say what you're working on, if you like, and how long you've got. Not sure where to send them? Ask it to suggest a plan for that much time.

  2. Leave it

    The timer is the whole screen. Nothing moves, nothing pays out, and there's nothing to do here. Lock your phone.

  3. Come back

    You get one notification when the run's done. Then you see what they found, with the full report a tap away.

Real screens from the app, with sample data.

Opening the app doesn't help them

Nothing piles up while you watch the timer. There's nothing to tap and nothing to collect. Every run starts at 100% Integrity, and you get 30 seconds to put the phone down. After that, every 30 seconds on the timer screen costs 1%, and every time you reopen Telos costs 4%. Locking your phone costs nothing, and neither does glancing at the Lock Screen countdown.

Losing Integrity trims what they bring home a little and your odds of finding gear a lot. It stops at 25%, and a run never fails.

What it can't see

Telos only knows about its own screen. It can't tell whether you went back to work or over to another app, and it doesn't block anything. That part is on the honor system. There are no leaderboards and no prizes, so there's nobody to cheat but yourself.

Where they go depends on how long you've got

Every place has a minimum. Come back before it and the squad brings home scraps. So one 90 minute block really is different from three 30s, but nothing in the game tells you which one you should be working. New places open as your guild grows and brings home intel.

  1. Mosswood Verge

    10 min

    Overgrown perimeter. Low risk, quick returns.

  2. Blackstone Hollow

    25 min

    Collapsed extraction works. Alloy-rich, slow going.

  3. The Cinder Archive

    40 min

    A burned library that never finished burning.

  4. Riftline Descent

    60 min

    Past the last marked map. Only a long run gets anywhere.

There's one more past those. You'll have to get there yourself.

If you only ever do 25s, that's fine. You'll stay in the first two places, and you'll keep finding gear, building your camp and reading what's there. The deeper places stay out of reach, and the app won't nag you about it.

Between runs, camp is yours

This is where the game is, if you want one. Spend what they brought home on gear, recruits and upgrades to the Forge, the Archive and the Barracks. Take on contracts. See exactly what a piece of gear changes before you swap it. Name your guild and pick its sigil.

None of it earns anything by itself. Only the next run does. So there's a point where you're done, and when you just want to work, the same plan is one tap away.

There's a story, if you want it

Your guild isn't the first to work this ground. A run's report can carry one line from what the others left behind. It's shown once, in order, and you can skip every word without losing a thing. There's nothing to collect and nothing keeps count. A short run still gets its line.

Nothing in Telos fights anything. Your guild is a company of surveyors, paid to find out what things are and come back. No enemies, no casualties, and no run comes home a defeat, because you were working.

For when the hard part is starting

A timer does nothing for the person who meant to start at 9 and sat down at 11. So you can name the time you mean to start, and on those days a supply caravan comes through.

  1. 15 min before

    It's at the gate. Send a squad now and they leave with it.

  2. On the minute

    It goes. If you missed it, you get a notice that there's still work to be done.

  3. Up to an hour after

    Its tracks are fresh. A squad can still follow, and what it can bring back fades by the minute.

It carries six pieces of gear you can't get anywhere else, and a record of its own. A 15 minute run counts the same as a 90, because it rewards starting, not working longer. Missing it costs nothing you already have, a missed day leaves no mark on the next, and it stays off unless you turn it on.

Every session, kept

The field log keeps what you said you'd work on, for how long, and how it went. It counts days worked, not days in a row, so a month off leaves the number where it was.

If you ever want to show someone, there's a card for a post or a Story. It has your guild's name and your numbers on it, and never your task or your notes.

What it won't do

  • No account, no ads, no analytics. Telos makes no network connections of its own, and it works offline.
  • What you write stays on your phone unless you choose to send it somewhere. Share cards never include your task or reflection notes.
  • No streaks and no reminders you didn't set up yourself. Take a month off and nothing's lost.
  • You'll never be able to buy progress. No boosts, no skipping a session, no energy. Focus is the only thing that moves the game.
  • The beta is free. If paid content comes later, it'll be more places to go, not a faster way to get there.
  • You can export your whole save to a file any time. Deleting the app deletes everything it keeps on your phone.

Questions

I've never played an RPG. Is this for me?

Yes. It's a timer before it's a game. You can tap through the first run with the plan it fills in for you, and camp is there if you get curious. There's nothing to learn before you can start working.

Will it block my other apps?

No. Telos can't block other apps or see them. It only knows how long you spent on its own screen and how many times you came back to it.

What if I have to stop early?

Recall the squad. They bring home what the time earned, or scraps if you stopped before that place's minimum. For the first 30 seconds you can cancel outright.

What if the app closes or my phone restarts?

The run keeps time anyway, because it's worked out from when you started. What they found is settled once, so force-quitting won't get you a different result.

Is it on Android?

iPhone only for now.
