More about it

What a run looks like

  1. Say what you're working on and for how long.

  2. Then lock your phone.

  3. The Lock Screen shows the time left. Looking at it costs nothing.

  4. One notification when it's done. Then see what they found.

  5. Every session goes in your field log.

  6. Share a card if you like. It never shows your task or notes.

Integrity

Every run starts at 100%. Watching the timer lowers it a little, reopening the app lowers it more, and locking your phone doesn't lower it at all. The higher it stays, the better your odds of finding gear. Telos can't see your other apps, so the rest is on the honor system.

No accounts, no ads, no streaks

  • Telos has no analytics and works offline. What you write stays on your phone unless you choose to send it somewhere.
  • It won't remind you about anything you didn't set up yourself. Take a month off and nothing's lost.
  • You'll never be able to buy progress. Focus is the only thing that moves the game.
