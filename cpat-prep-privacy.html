Privacy Policy

CPAT Prep collects no personal data. This policy explains exactly what that means.

Effective: April 15, 2026

The short version: CPAT Prep does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

1. Who We Are

CPAT Prep is developed and published by DahVio Studios, a solo developer studio based in Long Beach, California, operated by Ray (ray@dahvio.com).

2. Data We Do Not Collect

CPAT Prep does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, address)
  • Health or fitness data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Crash reports or diagnostics
  • Any information whatsoever

3. Local Storage

CPAT Prep saves your training data — including your fitness level, equipment preference, test date, event confidence ratings, weak area selections, and workout log history — to your device's local storage via iOS's standard UserDefaults system. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to DahVio Studios or any third party
  • Can be cleared at any time by deleting the app

4. Local Notifications

If you set a CPAT test date, the app may request permission to send local notifications as your test approaches. These notifications:

  • Are scheduled entirely on-device — no server is involved
  • Do not transmit any data
  • Can be disabled at any time in iOS Settings › Notifications › CPAT Prep

5. No Internet Connection Required

CPAT Prep operates entirely offline. The app makes no network requests of any kind. All training plans, workout logic, and event scoring are computed locally on your device.

6. No Third-Party SDKs or Analytics

CPAT Prep does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting frameworks. There are no SDKs from Google, Facebook, Firebase, or any other provider embedded in the app.

7. Children's Privacy

CPAT Prep is intended for adults preparing for the Candidate Physical Ability Test. The app is rated 4+ on the App Store. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

8. App Store Data Practices

While DahVio Studios collects no data, Apple may collect certain app usage data as part of normal App Store operations. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details on their data practices.

9. Changes to This Policy

If our data practices ever change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date and noted in the app's release notes. Given the offline, no-tracking nature of this app, material changes are unlikely.
