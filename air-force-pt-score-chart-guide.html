PFA Score Guide

Air Force PT score chart basics without bouncing through the whole manual.

If you are looking for the Air Force PT score chart, the first thing most people want to know is simpler than the full table: what score is passing, what score is Excellent, and which event is worth the most.

Quick Scores

The score ranges most people are looking for.

Excellent

90+

Excellent is the top overall category for the PFA composite score.

Satisfactory

75+

Satisfactory is the standard passing threshold most people mean when they ask what score they need.

Marginal

60-74

Marginal is below the normal passing benchmark and not where most people want to land.

Unsatisfactory

<60

Unsatisfactory is the lowest overall rating range.

Exact point values still depend on age, sex, event selection, and the applicable scoring table under current DAFMAN 36-2905 standards.

How To Read It

What the score chart is really helping you answer.

  • How close am I to a 75 or a 90?
  • Which event gives me the fastest path to more points?
  • Does trimming run time help more than adding reps?
  • What score do I need in one event to protect the overall composite?

The short version

  • The aerobic event usually moves the score the most.
  • Push-ups and core still matter, especially when you are near a category cutoff.
  • A calculator is easier than scanning the score chart when you want to test different outcomes quickly.
