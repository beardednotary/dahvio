Privacy Policy

BriefBuilder keeps your accomplishment log and EPB statements on your device. This policy explains what stays there, what is sent off the device when you use the AI writer, what the referral program records, and who processes it.

Effective: November 17, 2026

The short version: BriefBuilder has no account and no analytics. Your log and statements are stored only on your device. When you tap Write on an entry, that one entry and your rank, AFSC and component are sent to an AI service to generate statements. DahVio Studios does not store them. The only thing DahVio Studios keeps is a small referral record tied to an anonymous ID: your referral code and who joined with it.

1. Who We Are

BriefBuilder is developed and published by DahVio Studios, a solo developer studio based in Long Beach, California, operated by Ray (ray@dahvio.com).

BriefBuilder is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the U.S. Air Force, the Department of Defense, or the myEval system.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

BriefBuilder stores the following in the app's local storage on your device:

  • Your profile: rank, AFSC, component, and reporting period end date, plus your name if you choose to enter one
  • Your log entries, including the text you typed and the performance area, leadership quality, and proficiency level you tagged
  • Statements the AI writer generated and the ones you selected
  • The contents of your four EPB blocks
  • A count of how many free AI writes you have used, and whether onboarding is complete
  • Your reminder settings, whether you have agreed to entries being sent to the AI service, and whether you have shared or used a referral code

This data is not uploaded to a DahVio server, is not tied to an account, and is not synced or backed up by DahVio Studios. If you delete the app or lose your device, DahVio Studios cannot recover it.

3. What Is Sent When You Use the AI Writer

Writing statements requires sending text off your device. Before the first time, the app asks for your permission. After that, text is sent only when you tap the button to write or regenerate statements for an entry. Each time, the app sends:

  • The text of that one entry (your freeform text, or the action, outcome, metrics, and context fields)
  • The performance area, leadership quality, and proficiency level tagged on that entry
  • Your rank, AFSC, AFSC title, and component
  • The anonymous app user ID described in section 5

The app does not send your name, your other log entries, your EPB blocks, or any device or advertising identifier.

The request goes to a small relay service operated by DahVio Studios and hosted on Cloudflare. The relay forwards the entry and profile details to Anthropic, the company that provides the Claude AI model, and returns the generated statements to your device. The anonymous app user ID is not sent to Anthropic.

What the relay keeps

The relay does not store the content of your entries or the generated statements, and DahVio Studios keeps no database of them. The relay uses the anonymous app user ID only to check whether you joined with a friend's referral code; the first time you write a statement after joining that way, it notes that in the referral record so your friend earns their reward (see section 5). It keeps no other record of your writes.

To prevent abuse, the relay limits how many requests an IP address can make per minute; your IP address is processed for that purpose and for routine network operation by Cloudflare.

What Anthropic does with it

Anthropic processes the request to generate the statements, under its commercial terms and privacy policy. Under those terms Anthropic does not use content sent through its API to train its models, and it may retain requests for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy for details.

4. Do Not Enter Sensitive Information

Never enter classified information, controlled unclassified information (CUI), operational details that are not releasable, or personal information about other people. BriefBuilder is not an approved system for that material. Write entries the way your finished EPB will read: unclassified and releasable.

You are responsible for what you type into the app and for following your unit's and the Air Force's rules on information handling and the use of AI tools.

5. Referrals

Every install of BriefBuilder has a six-character referral code, shown on the Profile tab. A friend who enters your code gets a free month of Pro, and you earn a month when that friend writes their first statement.

Because there are no accounts, the referral program identifies each install by an anonymous app user ID. RevenueCat (see section 8) generates this ID at random when the app is first opened. It is not your name, email, phone number, Apple ID, or a device or advertising identifier.

What the referral record contains

DahVio Studios keeps a referral database on Cloudflare. A record is created the first time the Profile tab shows your code, or when you enter a friend's code. It stores:

  • The anonymous app user ID and the referral code assigned to it, with the date it was created
  • If you entered a friend's code: a record linking your anonymous ID to theirs, the date you entered it, and whether and when you went on to write your first statement
  • How many months of Pro you have earned through referrals, and how many have been applied

It does not contain your name, email address, contacts, log entries, or statements, and DahVio Studios cannot tell from it who you are.

Sharing your code

When you share your code, the app opens your device's standard share sheet with a short message containing the code and a link to the app. You choose who receives it. DahVio Studios does not see who you send it to.

How rewards are applied

Reward months are applied as promotional access through RevenueCat. To do that, the relay sends RevenueCat your anonymous app user ID and the date the reward should end, and asks RevenueCat whether you already have an active subscription. If you do, earned months are held in the referral record and applied after your subscription ends.

6. No Account. No Analytics. No Ads.

BriefBuilder has no sign-in and no user accounts. The app does not include:

  • Analytics frameworks
  • Advertising SDKs or ad identifiers
  • Behavioral tracking tools

DahVio Studios does not receive a record of how often you open the app, what you log, or which statements you choose. The referral record in section 5 is the only data about you that DahVio Studios keeps.

7. Reminders and Review Prompts

If you turn on reminders, BriefBuilder schedules them on your device as local notifications. They are not sent from a server, and the app does not request or store a push notification token. The reminder settings are stored on your device with the rest of your data. You can turn reminders off in the Profile tab or in your device's notification settings.

The app may occasionally ask Apple or Google to show the standard in-app rating prompt. Whether it appears, and anything you submit through it, is handled by Apple or Google.

8. Purchases

If you buy an upgrade, the payment is handled by Apple or Google through your App Store or Google Play account. DahVio Studios never sees your payment card details.

BriefBuilder uses RevenueCat to confirm whether a purchase or referral reward is active, to restore purchases, and to apply referral rewards. RevenueCat receives the anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this app, and any promotional access granted through referrals. It does not receive your log entries or statements. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

9. Resetting or Deleting Your Data

You can delete an individual log entry from the Log tab. To erase everything stored on your device at once, open the Profile tab and use Reset App Data. Deleting the app from your device also removes all locally stored data.

DahVio Studios keeps no server-side copy of your log or statements, so there is nothing further to delete for those. Resetting the app does not remove your referral record. To have it deleted, email ray@dahvio.com with the referral code shown on your Profile tab. Deleting the record cancels any reward months that have not been applied yet; Pro time already applied stays in place until it ends.

Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat under their own policies. If you would like your RevenueCat record deleted, include that in your email and DahVio Studios will request it.

10. Children's Privacy

BriefBuilder is intended for enlisted members of the U.S. Air Force. It is not directed to children under 13, and DahVio Studios does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Apple's and Google's Data Practices

Apple and Google may collect certain information as part of normal device, App Store, and Google Play operations. For details, see Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

12. Changes to This Policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
