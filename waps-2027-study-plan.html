WAPS 2027 8-week study planner

This is a simple pacing guide for E5 and E6 Airmen who want structure without building a full spreadsheet. The idea is consistency: AFH-1 review, quick knowledge checks, and steady SJT reps instead of giant cramming sessions.

Week 1: Build the baseline

Figure out what already feels familiar and what clearly does not.

  • Skim the AFH-1 sections that will drive most of your review.
  • Run a short mixed quiz or quick drill to get an honest starting point.
  • Do one dedicated SJT block and note why your misses happened.

Week 2: Lock in a rhythm

Choose the study cadence you can repeat when life gets busy.

  • Two AFH-1 review sessions focused on weak sections.
  • Three short flashcard or quiz sessions.
  • One SJT session with notes on leadership themes.

Week 3: Start second-pass review

Revisit the sections that did not stick the first time.

  • Review flagged concepts instead of rereading everything.
  • Run timed question sets to improve recall speed.
  • Keep SJT separate so judgment patterns stay visible.

Week 4: Audit the weak areas

Midpoint is where the study plan should tighten up.

  • List the AFH-1 topics that still feel shaky.
  • Check whether your misses come from knowledge gaps or rushing.
  • Repeat the same SJT categories until the patterns make sense.

Week 5: Increase pressure

Do longer sets and reduce how often you pause to look things up.

  • One longer mixed drill or practice test.
  • AFH-1 review only for sections that still cause misses.
  • One focused SJT session on least-effective answers.

Week 6: Tighten recall

This week is about retrieval, not endless rereading.

  • Use flashcards and section drills more than passive notes.
  • Practice explaining key concepts out loud.
  • Run SJT sets without second-guessing every item.

Week 7: Simulate test rhythm

Build comfort with the pace and mental switching.

  • Use timed quizzes or exam mode if available.
  • Review flagged and missed questions in one batch.
  • Do a final targeted SJT block on the weakest competency area.

Week 8: Calm, sharpen, repeat

The last week should feel controlled, not panicked.

  • Review summaries, flagged notes, and weak areas only.
  • Keep daily sessions short and intentional.
  • Do one final SJT pass focused on judgment and professionalism.
