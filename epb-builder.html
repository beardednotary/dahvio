EPB Basics

What an EPB asks you to write.

The Enlisted Performance Brief replaced the old EPR and its bullet format. Instead of abbreviated bullets, you write short narrative performance statements under four Major Performance Areas. Each area has one block, and each block holds up to 350 characters.

350 characters

Executing the Mission

Using knowledge, initiative, and adaptability to produce timely, high-quality results. Covers job proficiency, initiative, and adaptability.

350 characters

Leading People

Building cohesive teams, communicating clearly, and taking care of people. Covers inclusion and teamwork, emotional intelligence, and communication.

350 characters

Managing Resources

Managing time, equipment, people, and funds responsibly, and owning the results. Covers stewardship and accountability.

350 characters

Improving the Unit

Thinking critically and finding better ways to get the mission done. Covers decision making and innovation.

Those ten behaviors are the Airman Leadership Qualities. BriefBuilder lets you tag an entry with the quality it shows, and the writer aims the statement at it.

EPB Close-Out Dates

What Makes a Strong Statement

The rules the writer follows.

These come from the Air Force's own guidance on performance statements. They are also a useful checklist if you are writing your EPB by hand.

Action plus impact

Each statement stands alone as a complete sentence: what you did, and at least one result, impact, or outcome.

Plain language

No shorthand a reader outside your career field would have to decode. Uncommon acronyms get spelled out or dropped.

Numbers kept

People led, dollars saved, hours cut, percentages gained. If you logged a number, the statement uses it.

No clichés

Phrases like "go-to Airman" and "consummate professional" say nothing about what happened, so the writer leaves them out.

FAQ

A few direct answers.

Is BriefBuilder an official Air Force app?

No. BriefBuilder is an independent tool from DahVio Studios. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the U.S. Air Force, the Department of Defense, or myEval.

How long can an EPB performance statement be?

Each of the four Major Performance Area blocks holds up to 350 characters. Most Airmen fit two statements of roughly 150 to 175 characters into each block. BriefBuilder counts characters for every statement and every block.

Does it write old-style EPR bullets?

No. The EPB replaced bullet format with narrative performance statements: plain-language sentences that pair an action with its impact, result, or outcome. BriefBuilder writes in that narrative style.

Where is my EPB content stored?

Your log entries and statements are stored on your own device. There is no account. When you ask the app to write statements for an entry, that entry's text is sent to an AI service to generate them and is not stored by DahVio Studios. Details are in the privacy policy.

Should I put sensitive details in an entry?

No. Never enter classified information, controlled unclassified information, or other people's personal details. Write entries the way the finished EPB will read: unclassified and releasable.

Does it work for the Space Force?

No. BriefBuilder is built for the Air Force enlisted evaluation system: Regular Air Force, Air Force Reserve, and Air National Guard. Guardians are evaluated under a separate system.
