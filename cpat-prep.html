FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the CPAT?

The Candidate Physical Ability Test (CPAT) is a standardized physical fitness test required by many fire departments during the hiring process. It consists of eight timed events — Stair Climb, Hose Drag, Equipment Carry, Ladder Raise & Extension, Forcible Entry, Search, Rescue Drag, and Ceiling Breach & Pull — completed sequentially in under 10 minutes 20 seconds.

Is CPAT Prep an official app?

No. CPAT Prep is an unofficial training tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sanctioned by the IAFF, IAFC, or any fire department or testing agency. Always verify test requirements with your local fire department or testing provider.

How does the training plan work?

During onboarding you select your current fitness level, available equipment, and any weak areas. CPAT Prep generates a weekly training plan built around those inputs, cycling through workouts and event-specific timers that target the demands of each CPAT event. The plan updates automatically every week, and you can edit your profile any time to adjust it.

What do the event confidence ratings do?

Logging your confidence after each event — High, Okay, or Low — adjusts that event's status over time, helping the app surface your weakest areas as the focus of your training week.

Does CPAT Prep work offline?

Yes. CPAT Prep is fully offline — training plans, timers, event status, and workout history all run and save locally on your device. No account or internet connection is required.

Is there a subscription?

No. CPAT Prep is a one-time $1.99 purchase — no subscription, no ads, and no in-app purchases.
