Questions

What is the CPAT?

The Candidate Physical Ability Test is a standardized physical fitness test required by many fire departments during hiring. It's eight timed events — Stair Climb, Hose Drag, Equipment Carry, Ladder Raise & Extension, Forcible Entry, Search, Rescue Drag, and Ceiling Breach & Pull — completed sequentially in under 10 minutes 20 seconds.

Is CPAT Prep an official app?

No. CPAT Prep is an unofficial training tool, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sanctioned by the IAFF, IAFC, or any fire department or testing agency. Always verify test requirements with your local department or testing provider.

How does the training plan work?

During onboarding you set your current fitness level, the equipment you have access to, and any weak areas. CPAT Prep builds a weekly plan around those inputs, cycling through workouts and event-specific timers that target the demands of each event. The plan regenerates every week, and editing your profile adjusts it.

What do the event confidence ratings do?

Rating an event high, okay or low after you train it updates that event's status over time, which is what the app uses to surface your weakest areas as the focus of the training week.

Does CPAT Prep work offline?

Yes. Plans, timers, event status and workout history all run and save locally on your device. No account or internet connection is required.

Is there a subscription?

No. CPAT Prep is a one-time $1.99 purchase — no subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases.
