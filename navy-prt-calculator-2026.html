FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the Navy PRT?

The Navy Physical Readiness Test (PRT) is the fitness test for active duty and reserve Sailors. It has three events: push-ups, a forearm plank, and a cardio event (1.5-mile run, 2,000-meter row, 500-yard swim, or 450-meter swim), each scored from 45 to 100 points.

How is the Navy PRT scored?

Each event earns points from the table for your age group and sex, and the three are averaged. The average sets your category: Outstanding 90 to 100, Excellent 75 to 89, Good 60 to 74, and Satisfactory 45 to 59. Scoring below 45 on any event fails the PRT whatever the average.

What is a passing Navy PRT score?

Every event needs at least 45 points, the lowest listed score; below that is an event failure and fails the PRT. With all three events at 45 or better, the average sets your category, Satisfactory or higher.

How many push-ups do you need for the Navy PRT?

For 100 points, men aged 17 to 19 need 92 push-ups and men aged 20 to 24 need 87; women need 51 and 48. The 45-point minimum is 42 and 37 for men in those groups, and 19 and 16 for women. Every age group is on the score chart below.

How long do you have to hold the plank for the Navy PRT?

For 100 points, men aged 17 to 19 hold 3:24 and women 3:14; at 20 to 24 it is 3:20 and 3:10. The 45-point minimum is 1:11 for men and 1:01 for women aged 17 to 19.

What is the Navy PRT 1.5-mile run time?

Below 5,000 feet, 100 points is 8:15 for men and 9:29 for women aged 17 to 19, and 8:30 and 9:47 at 20 to 24. The slowest time that still scores 45 is 12:45 for men and 15:00 for women aged 17 to 19. At 5,000 feet and above a separate table allows more time.

Can you swim or row instead of run on the Navy PRT?

Yes. The calculator scores all four cardio options: the 1.5-mile run, 2,000-meter row, 500-yard swim, and 450-meter swim. Each has its own table, and the run has a separate table for testing at 5,000 feet and above.

What is the Navy body composition standard?

Under PRP Guide-4 (June 2026), Step 1 is a waist-to-height ratio of 0.5499 or lower, with height rounded up and waist rounded down to the nearest half inch. Over that, Step 2 measures body fat against 26% for men and 36% for women. Check yours on the waist-to-height calculator.

What instruction governs the Navy PRT?

OPNAVINST 6110.1L sets the Navy Physical Readiness Program. The PRT scoring tables are in PRP Guide-5A, Physical Readiness Test, Tables 4-1 and 4-2.

Is this an official Navy PRT calculator?

No. It is an independent tool scored on the official PRT tables, not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Navy or Department of Defense. Your command fitness leader records the official score.
