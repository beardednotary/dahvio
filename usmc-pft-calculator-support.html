Frequently Asked Questions

Is this app official?

No. USMC PFT Calculator is an unofficial reference tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sanctioned by the United States Marine Corps. All scores should be verified against official MCO 6100.13A guidelines.

Which scoring standards does the app use?

The app uses the current MCO 6100.13A scoring tables for the Marine Corps Physical Fitness Test (PFT). Scoring tables are updated with each app release to reflect the latest Marine Corps Order.

My score looks wrong. What should I check?

Verify your age, gender, and the correct event type (e.g. pull-ups vs. push-ups). If you hold a combat arms PMOS, make sure the Combat Arms toggle is on: since January 1, 2026 you are scored on the male tables and held to the 210-point combat arms standard. If you still believe there's an error, please contact us.

What is the Combat Arms MOS toggle?

Since January 1, 2026 (MARADMIN 613/25), Marines with a combat arms PMOS (infantry, artillery, assault amphibian, combat engineer, and EOD specialties) are scored on the male, age-normed tables and must reach 210 points. Turning the toggle on scores you on those tables and shows whether you meet 210. A combat arms Marine who passes but scores under 210 still passes, but goes on remedial PT and may face PMOS reclassification or promotion restriction.

How does body composition affect my score?

Since January 1, 2026 (MARADMIN 066/26, as changed by 073/26), the Marine Corps screens body composition by waist-to-height ratio instead of height and weight. Enter your height and your waist at the navel; both are rounded down to the nearest half inch, and a ratio of 0.52 or less is within standards. If you are over, enter your body fat percentage from the tape test or BIA, plus your CFT score if you have one, so the app can apply the 250+ and 285+ PFT/CFT allowances. The app marks the PFT as failed when body composition is not within standards.

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. USMC PFT Calculator is fully offline. All scoring logic runs locally on your device. No data is ever transmitted.

Will my inputs be saved if I close the app?

Yes. Your last entered values are automatically saved and restored the next time you open the app. No account required.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to submit a refund request directly through the App Store.
