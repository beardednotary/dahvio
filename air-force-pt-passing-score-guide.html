Passing Score Guide

What you need to pass the Air Force PFA

A composite of 75 out of 100 is Satisfactory, which is a pass, as long as every component scores above zero. 90 or more is Excellent. Anything under 75, or a zero in any one component, is Unsatisfactory.

Ratings

Three ratings, one composite.

The 2026 PFA adds four components into a single score out of 100 and gives it one of three ratings.

Excellent

90+

Composite of 90.0 or higher, with every component above zero.

Satisfactory

75–89.9

The passing band, again with every component above zero.

Unsatisfactory

<75

A composite under 75, or a zero in any single component, whatever the total.

Where a few points usually come from.

Cardio has the most room

A scoring run or HAMR is worth 35 to 50 points, so a faster 2-mile time or a few more laps usually moves the composite more than extra reps.

Body composition is measured

A waist-to-height ratio of 0.49 or less earns all 20 points. The points step down from there and reach zero at 0.60.

Clear every floor first

A zero in any component fails the assessment even with a composite over 75, so a component near its minimum is the first thing to fix.
