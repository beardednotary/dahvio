Passing Score Guide

What score do you need to pass the Air Force PT test?

The short answer is that a Satisfactory result starts at 75 points. Most people asking this question also want to know what counts as Excellent, how much margin they really have, and which event is usually the fastest place to make up points.

Quick Answer

The score bands most people are looking for.

Excellent

90+

Excellent is the top overall rating band for the Air Force PFA.

Satisfactory

75+

Satisfactory is the normal passing threshold.

Marginal

60-74

Marginal is below the normal passing benchmark.

Unsatisfactory

<60

Unsatisfactory is the lowest overall rating range.

What That Means

Passing is about the full composite score.

The Air Force PFA is not just about one event. Your result comes from the full composite score, with aerobic fitness carrying the heaviest weight. That is why so many people can improve the total faster through the run or HAMR than through small changes in the other components.

  • If you are below 75, the fastest path up is often the aerobic event.
  • If you are sitting in the low 70s, even a modest improvement can move you into Satisfactory.
  • If you are already above 75, the question usually becomes whether you are trying to feel safer or push into Excellent.
