Marine Corps Order

MCO 6100.13A, explained

Marine Corps Order 6100.13A is the order behind the Physical Fitness Test (PFT) and Combat Fitness Test (CFT). It defines the events, the scoring tables, the age groups, the minimums, and the testing calendar. This page is a plain-language summary of what it contains — not a substitute for the order itself.

The PFT

Physical Fitness Test

The PFT measures general fitness with three events, each worth up to 100 points for a 300-point maximum:

  • Upper body — dead-hang pull-ups or push-ups. Choosing push-ups caps this event at 70 points, so a perfect 300 requires pull-ups.
  • Core — the plank. Crunches were removed; the plank is now the only core event, scored on time held.
  • Endurance — a 3-mile run for time.

Age groups: 17–20, 21–25, 26–30, 31–35, 36–40, 41–45, 46–50, and 51+. Standards adjust by age and gender.

PFT Classes

How the total is graded

ClassTotal score
First Class235 – 300
Second Class200 – 234
Third Class150 – 199
FailBelow 150, or any event below its minimum

The 150-point floor is the overall minimum. Each event also has an age- and gender-based minimum you have to clear on its own.

The CFT

Combat Fitness Test

The CFT measures functional, combat-related fitness in utilities. It also has three events scored to a 300-point maximum, using the same First / Second / Third Class bands:

  • Movement to Contact (MTC) — an 880-yard timed run.
  • Ammunition Lift — a 30-lb ammo can pressed overhead, max repetitions in 2 minutes.
  • Maneuver Under Fire (MANUF) — a timed 300-yard shuttle combining sprints, a buddy drag and carry, an ammo-can carry, a grenade throw, and agility movements.
Administration

Testing rules under the order

Frequency

One PFT and one CFT per calendar year — typically PFT in the first half, CFT in the second.

Semiannual spacing

The two tests are separated so Marines are assessed across the year, not back to back.

Partial tests

The order covers partial PFT/CFT credit for Marines medically unable to complete every event.

Consequences

Failures and first/second/third class results feed reporting, body composition, and remedial-program requirements.

FAQ

Common questions

What is MCO 6100.13A?

The Marine Corps Order governing the Physical Fitness Program — it sets the standards, scoring tables, and rules for both the PFT and the CFT.

What events are in the PFT?

Pull-ups or push-ups, the plank, and a 3-mile run. Push-ups cap the upper-body event at 70 of 100 points.

What is a first class PFT score?

235 to 300. Second class is 200–234, third class is 150–199, and 150 is the overall minimum — with per-event minimums on top.

How often is the test?

One PFT and one CFT per calendar year, spaced across the two halves of the year.
