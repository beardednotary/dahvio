How it works

Dump it. Decide. Let it go.

Most journals are built to help you remember. This one is built for the thoughts you're done carrying.

FAQ

A few quick answers.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in and no cloud profile. Everything you write or record stays on your iPhone.

Is burning really permanent?

Yes. Burning deletes the entry and its voice note from the app. There's no trash folder and no undo, so the app asks you once before it burns.

Wasn't this called Ember Journal?

It was. Same app, new name. Your entries and any Premium purchase carry over with the update.

Questions or bugs?

Email ray@dahvio.com or visit the support page. What happened, what device, what you expected: that's enough to go on.
