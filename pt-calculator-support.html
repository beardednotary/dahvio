Frequently Asked Questions

Is this app official?

No. PT Calculator is an unofficial reference tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sanctioned by the United States Air Force or United States Space Force. All scores should be verified against official DAFMAN 36-2905 guidelines.

Which scoring standards does the app use?

The app uses the 2026 DAFMAN 36-2905 scoring tables for the Air Force Physical Fitness Assessment (PFA) and Space Force Human Performance Assessment (HPA). Scoring tables are updated with each app release.

My score looks wrong. What should I check?

Verify your age, gender, and the correct event type (e.g. traditional push-ups vs. hand-release). Altitude group also affects run scoring — set this to "None" unless your base is in a designated high-altitude group. If you still believe there's an error, please contact us.

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. PT Calculator is fully offline. All scoring logic runs locally on your device. No data is ever transmitted.

Will my inputs be saved if I close the app?

Yes. Your last entered values are automatically saved and restored the next time you open the app. No account required.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to submit a refund request directly through the App Store.
