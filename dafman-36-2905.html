DAFMAN 36-2905, explained

DAFMAN 36-2905 is the Department of the Air Force Physical Fitness Program manual. It replaced AFI 36-2905 and is the single source for the Air Force and Space Force fitness assessment — the components, the exercise options, the composite scoring charts, the age groups, and the rules. This page is a plain-language summary.

Components

Four scored components, one composite

ComponentOptionsComposite weight
Cardio2-mile run or 20 m HAMR shuttle50 points
Body compositionWaist-to-height ratio20 points
Strength1-min push-ups or 2-min hand-release push-ups15 points
Core1-min sit-ups, 2-min cross-leg reverse crunch, or forearm plank15 points

You pick one exercise for cardio, strength and core; body composition is measured. Each has its own chart by sex and age group in the manual. A waist-to-height ratio of 0.49 or less earns all 20 body composition points.

Scoring

Passing, Excellent, and the component minimums

Excellent

Composite 90.0 or higher, with every component above zero.

Satisfactory

Composite 75.0 to 89.9, with every component above zero. This is the passing band.

Unsatisfactory

Composite below 75.0, or a zero in any single component.

Component minimums

Below its minimum, a component scores zero. A scoring cardio result is worth at least 35 points; strength and core start at 2.5. Clearing 75 with one zero is still an Unsatisfactory.

Age groups run in five-year bands (under 25, 25–29, 30–34, and so on). Cardio results at higher-elevation installations are adjusted by altitude group.

Also In The Manual

Rules beyond the score

  • Test frequency — how often you assess and when, with administrative timing set by your unit fitness program manager.
  • Exemptions — component and full exemptions for medical conditions, with the assessment scored on the remaining components where allowed.
  • Altitude adjustment — cardio results are adjusted at designated installations above 5,000 feet, by altitude group.
  • Space Force — Guardians assess under the same manual for now.
  • Failures — consequences, reassessment timelines, and required fitness intervention programs.
FAQ

Common questions

What is DAFMAN 36-2905?

The Department of the Air Force Physical Fitness Program manual. It replaced AFI 36-2905 and governs the fitness assessment for the Air Force and Space Force.

What are the PFA components?

Cardio (2-mile run or HAMR, 50 points), body composition (waist-to-height ratio, 20), strength (push-ups or hand-release push-ups, 15), and core (sit-ups, cross-leg reverse crunch, or plank, 15) — combined into a 100-point composite.

What is a passing score?

A composite of 75.0 or higher with every component above zero. 90.0+ is Excellent.

Does it apply to the Space Force?

Yes, Guardians currently assess under the same manual.
