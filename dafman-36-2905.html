DAF Manual

DAFMAN 36-2905, explained

DAFMAN 36-2905 is the Department of the Air Force Physical Fitness Program manual. It replaced AFI 36-2905 and is the single source for the Air Force and Space Force fitness assessment — the components, the exercise options, the composite scoring charts, the age groups, and the rules. This page is a plain-language summary.

Components

Three scored components, one composite

ComponentOptionsComposite weight
Aerobic1.5-mile run or 20 m HAMR shuttle60 points
Muscular fitness1-min push-ups or 2-min hand-release push-ups20 points
Core1-min sit-ups, cross-leg reverse crunch, or forearm plank20 points

You pick one exercise per component. Each has its own age- and gender-based chart in the manual.

Scoring

Passing, Excellent, and the component minimums

Excellent

Composite 90.0 or higher, with every component minimum met.

Satisfactory

Composite 75.0 to 89.9, with every component minimum met. This is the passing band.

Unsatisfactory

Composite below 75.0, or any single component below its minimum score.

Component minimums

Each component has a floor. Clearing the composite but missing one floor is still an Unsatisfactory.

Age groups run in five-year bands (under 25, 25–29, 30–34, and so on). Aerobic standards at higher-elevation installations use the altitude-adjusted tables.

Also In The Manual

Rules beyond the score

  • Test frequency — an official assessment once per year for most members, with administrative timing set by your unit fitness program manager.
  • Exemptions — component and full exemptions for medical conditions, with the assessment scored on the remaining components where allowed.
  • Altitude adjustment — run and HAMR standards are adjusted at designated installations above a set elevation.
  • Space Force — Guardians assess under the same manual for now.
  • Failures — consequences, reassessment timelines, and required fitness intervention programs.

FAQ

Common questions

What is DAFMAN 36-2905?

The Department of the Air Force Physical Fitness Program manual. It replaced AFI 36-2905 and governs the fitness assessment for the Air Force and Space Force.

What are the PFA components?

Aerobic (1.5-mile run or HAMR), muscular fitness (push-ups or hand-release push-ups), and core (sit-ups, cross-leg reverse crunch, or plank) — combined into a 100-point composite weighted 60/20/20.

What is a passing score?

A composite of 75.0 or higher with every component minimum met. 90.0+ is Excellent.

Does it apply to the Space Force?

Yes, Guardians currently assess under the same manual.
