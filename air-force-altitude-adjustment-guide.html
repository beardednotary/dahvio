Altitude Adjustment

Air Force PFA altitude adjustment

If you test at an installation above 5,000 feet, your cardio result is adjusted for altitude before it is scored. At lower elevations, nothing changes.

How it works

Altitude groups, not a single rule.

  • Installations at elevation are assigned an altitude group, numbered 1 to 4.
  • The higher the group, the larger the adjustment.
  • The adjusted result is then read against the normal chart for your sex and age group.
  • Body composition, strength and core are not adjusted.
What to do

Before you test at elevation.

Find your group

Your unit fitness program manager can tell you whether your installation has an altitude group, and which one.

Expect a slower raw time

If you train low and test high, your watch will read slower. The adjustment is there to offset that, so plan against the adjusted result.

Mind the margins

The adjustment matters most near a cutoff: the 75 for Satisfactory, the 90 for Excellent, or the cardio minimum.
