How The CFT Is Scored

CFT classes

Each event earns up to 100 points from the table for your age group and sex. The three add up to your total, and the total sets your class under MCO 6100.13A Table 3-2, the same cutoffs as the PFT.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a passing CFT score?

You need at least 150 of 300 points and the minimum on all three events: Movement to Contact, Ammunition Lift, and Maneuver Under Fire. Missing any one minimum fails the CFT whatever the total. Classes under MCO 6100.13A Table 3-2 are 1st Class 235 to 300, 2nd Class 200 to 234, and 3rd Class 150 to 199.

What does it take to max the CFT?

A perfect 300 means 100 points on each event. For men aged 21 to 25 that is a 2:38 Movement to Contact, 115 ammo can lifts, and a 2:04 Maneuver Under Fire. For women aged 21 to 25 it is 3:13, 74 lifts, and 2:45. The standards ease with age; the score chart lists every age group.

How many ammo can lifts do you need on the CFT?

For 100 points, men need 95 to 120 lifts depending on age group, and women need 44 to 75. To reach the event minimum, men need 62 to 67 lifts through age 50 (16 at 51 and over), and women need 30 through age 40, then 28, 26, and 6 in the older groups.

What is a good Movement to Contact time?

A 100-point Movement to Contact is 2:38 to 3:05 for men and 3:10 to 3:55 for women, depending on age group. The slowest passing time is 3:45 for men aged 17 to 25 and 4:36 for women aged 17 to 20.

What are the CFT events?

Three timed or counted events, each worth up to 100 points: Movement to Contact, an 880-yard sprint; Ammunition Lift, lifting a 30-pound ammo can from shoulder height to overhead as many times as possible in 2 minutes; and Maneuver Under Fire, a 300-yard shuttle with crawls, a buddy drag and carry, an ammo can carry, a grenade throw, and agility running.

When do Marines take the CFT?

Active duty Marines take the CFT once each calendar year between 1 July and 31 December under MCO 6100.13A. The PFT is taken between 1 January and 30 June.

Does the 2026 combat arms standard apply to the CFT?

No. MARADMIN 613/25, effective 1 January 2026, scores combat arms PMOSs on the male PFT tables and requires at least 210 points. It covers the PFT. The CFT is still scored on your own age and sex tables.

Does my CFT score affect body composition?

It can. A Marine over the 0.52 waist-to-height ratio goes to a body fat evaluation. Scoring 250 or more on both the PFT and CFT adds 1% to the body fat limit, and 285 or more on both allows up to 26% for men or 36% for women (MARADMIN 066/26).

Is this an official Marine Corps CFT calculator?

No. It is an independent tool scored on the official MCO 6100.13A tables. The score of record is the one your unit enters in MCTIMS.
