FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the Air Force PFA?

The Air Force Physical Fitness Assessment measures overall fitness through body composition, an aerobic event, push-ups, and a core strength component for Air Force and Space Force members.

How is the Air Force PT test scored?

The PFA produces a composite score out of 100 points: aerobic fitness (2-mile run or HAMR) 50, waist-to-height ratio 20, push-ups 15, and core 15. A composite of 90 or above is Excellent and 75 or above is Satisfactory; below 75, or zero points on any component, is Unsatisfactory.

What score do I need to pass the Air Force fitness test?

A composite score of 75 or higher earns a Satisfactory result under current DAFMAN 36-2905 standards.

What governs the Air Force fitness assessment?

The Air Force PFA is governed by DAFMAN 36-2905, the Department of the Air Force manual that defines standards, scoring tables, and testing procedures.

Does this calculator work for Space Force?

Yes. The app includes support for both Air Force and Space Force standards under DAFMAN 36-2905.

Is this calculator affiliated with the Air Force or Space Force?

No. This is an independent tool for reference and training purposes only. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Air Force, U.S. Space Force, or Department of Defense.
