Simple Reality

Most people gain points fastest through the aerobic event.

Training Insight

Because aerobic fitness carries the highest weight in the PFA composite score, trimming time off the 2-mile run often changes the total faster than small gains in other events.

That does not make push-ups or core unimportant. It just helps explain why so many Airmen check the run first when they are trying to move from one rating band to another.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the Air Force PFA?

The Air Force Physical Fitness Assessment (PFA) measures fitness with four components: body composition (waist-to-height ratio), an aerobic event (2-mile run or HAMR), a strength event, and a core event.

How is the Air Force PT test scored?

The PFA produces a composite score out of 100 points: aerobic fitness (2-mile run or HAMR) 50, waist-to-height ratio 20, push-ups 15, and core 15. A composite of 90 or above is Excellent and 75 or above is Satisfactory; below 75, or zero points on any component, is Unsatisfactory.

What is a passing Air Force PT score?

A composite of 75 or higher with points on every component is Satisfactory under DAFMAN 36-2905, and 90 or higher is Excellent. Zero points on any one component makes the PFA Unsatisfactory whatever the total.

How many push-ups do you need for the Air Force PT test?

For all 15 points, men under 25 need 67 push-ups and women under 25 need 50; at 25 to 29 it is 63 and 47. The fewest that score any points are 30 for men and 15 for women under 25. Every age group is on the score chart below.

What 2-mile run time do you need for the Air Force PT test?

For all 50 points, men under 25 need 13:25 or faster and women 15:30; at 25 to 29 it is 13:35 and 15:55. The slowest times that still score are 19:45 for men and 25:23 for women under 25.

What is the waist-to-height ratio for the Air Force PFA?

Body composition is 20 of the 100 points. A ratio of 0.49 or lower earns all 20, each 0.01 above that costs 1 to 2.5 points, and 0.60 or higher earns none, which makes the PFA Unsatisfactory. Check yours on the waist-to-height calculator.

What governs the Air Force fitness assessment?

The Air Force PFA is governed by DAFMAN 36-2905, the Department of the Air Force manual that defines standards, scoring tables, and testing procedures.

Does this calculator work for Space Force?

Yes. Guardians currently assess under DAFMAN 36-2905, so the same scoring applies.

Is this an official Air Force PT calculator?

No. It is an independent tool scored on the DAFMAN 36-2905 charts, not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Air Force, U.S. Space Force, or Department of Defense. Your Fitness Assessment Cell records the official score.
