Classes

How the PFT and CFT are graded

Each event is worth up to 100 points (push-ups top out at 70). The three events add up to a total out of 300, and the same cutoffs set the class on both tests (Tables 2-1 and 3-2).

ClassTotal score
First Class235 – 300
Second Class200 – 234
Third Class150 – 199
FailBelow 150, or any event below its minimum

Since 1 January 2026, Marines in a combat arms PMOS take the PFT on the male tables whatever their sex and must reach 210 (MARADMIN 613/25). The CFT is still scored on each Marine's own tables. The PFT runs 1 January to 30 June and the CFT 1 July to 31 December.

FAQ

Common questions

How many pull-ups do you need for a perfect PFT score?

Men need 18 to 23 pull-ups for 100 points depending on age group (23 from age 21 to 35). Women need 4 to 12 (12 from age 26 to 30).

What is the minimum number of pull-ups for the Marine PFT?

The event minimum is 3 to 5 pull-ups for men and 1 to 4 for women, depending on age group. Below the minimum fails the PFT.

What is the 3-mile run standard for the Marine PFT?

100 points is 18:00 for men and 21:00 for women through age 40, easing to 19:30 and 22:30 at 51 and over. The slowest passing time is 27:40 for men aged 17 to 25 and 30:50 for women aged 17 to 25.

What is the plank standard for the Marine PFT?

The plank table is the same for every Marine: a 3:45 hold earns 100 points and 1:10 is the 40-point minimum.

How do you read the Marine PFT score chart?

Find your sex and age group, then the row for the points you want. The cell shows the fewest reps or the slowest time that earns those points. A time between two listed times scores as the slower one. A dash means no performance lands on exactly that point value.
