Body Composition 2026

Waist-to-height ratio check

Since January 1, 2026 the Marine Corps screens body composition by waist-to-height ratio instead of height and weight: 0.52 or less, the same for men and women (MARADMIN 066/26, as changed by 073/26). Enter your height and your waist measured at the navel.

Your maximum waist for your height appears here.

Both measurements are rounded down to the nearest 1/2 inch, as the MARADMIN directs, and checked against its height and maximum waist table. Over the limit means a body fat evaluation (tape test or bioelectrical impedance) against the MCO 6110.3A body fat standards. Marines with 250+ on both the PFT and CFT get an extra 1%; 285+ on both allows up to 26% (male) or 36% (female). Unofficial tool: your unit's measurement is the record.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the Marine PFT?

The Marine Corps Physical Fitness Test (PFT) measures overall physical readiness through pull-ups or push-ups, core strength (the plank; crunches were discontinued at the end of 2022), and a 3-mile run. It is a mandatory assessment for all active duty and reserve Marines.

What is a good Marine PFT score?

A score of 235 or higher is typically considered first class, though exact classifications depend on age and gender according to official MCO 6100.13A standards. A perfect score is 300 points.

How is the Marine PFT scored?

Each event is assigned a point value based on performance. The total score is the sum of all three events, with a maximum of 300 points—100 per event. Point values are determined by MCO 6100.13A scoring tables.

What order governs the Marine PFT?

The Marine PFT is governed by Marine Corps Order MCO 6100.13A, which outlines scoring standards, testing procedures, and requirements for all Marines.

Can I use pull-ups or push-ups for the PFT?

Yes. Marines can choose either pull-ups or push-ups for the upper body portion of the PFT. Point values differ between the two options per MCO 6100.13A guidelines. The calculator handles both.

Is this calculator affiliated with the Marine Corps?

No. This is an independent tool for reference and training purposes only. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Marine Corps or Department of Defense.

PFT Score Chart

Marine PFT classes at a glance

Your three event scores add up to a total out of 300, and that total sets your PFT class under MCO 6100.13A. Each event also has its own age- and gender-based minimum.

Class Total score
First Class235 – 300
Second Class200 – 234
Third Class150 – 199
FailBelow 150, or any event below its minimum

Combat arms (from January 1, 2026): under MARADMIN 613/25, Marines with a combat arms PMOS are scored on the male, age-normed tables whatever their sex, and must reach 210. A combat arms Marine who passes with less than 210 keeps their class but goes on remedial PT and may face PMOS reclassification or promotion restriction.
