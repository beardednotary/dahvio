AFH-1 study outline for WAPS prep

Use this page as a review map, not a textbook replacement. The point is to break AFH-1 prep into sections you can rate honestly, revisit on purpose, and connect to question practice instead of rereading the same material without a plan.

Section 1: First-pass review

Read for structure, not memorization. Your goal here is figuring out what the section is about and which terms or concepts are new.

  • What are the main ideas this section keeps returning to?
  • Which concepts would be easy to confuse on a question set?
  • What do you need to turn into flashcards or short notes?

Section 2: Drill review

After the first read, run questions on the same material. Do not judge the section only by how it felt while reading.

  • Which items did you miss because you did not know the content?
  • Which misses came from rushing or overthinking?
  • What should be flagged for second-pass review?
