Frequently Asked Questions

Is this app official?

No. Navy PRT Calculator is an unofficial reference tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sanctioned by the United States Navy. All scores should be verified against official OPNAVINST 6110.1J guidelines.

Which scoring standards does the app use?

The app uses the current OPNAVINST 6110.1J scoring tables for the Navy Physical Readiness Test (PRT). Scoring tables are updated with each app release to reflect the latest instruction.

My score looks wrong. What should I check?

Verify your age, gender, and the correct cardio event type (1.5-mile run, swim, stationary bike, or rowing machine). Each option uses different scoring tables. If you still believe there's an error, please contact us.

How does body composition (BCA) affect my score?

Sailors must pass the Body Composition Assessment before a PRT score is valid. The app checks height/weight standards first. If you exceed the weight limit for your height, a tape test body fat percentage is required. Failing BCA results in an administrative PRT failure regardless of your numeric score.

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Navy PRT Calculator is fully offline. All scoring logic runs locally on your device. No data is ever transmitted.

Will my inputs be saved if I close the app?

Yes. Your last entered values are automatically saved and restored the next time you open the app. No account required.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to submit a refund request directly through the App Store.
